## 1. The position (one line)

> **A Chartered Tax Adviser-led accountancy practice for UK business owners who need decisions planned before the money moves.**

General practice, specific spikes. Homepage sells the common problem, service and search pages sell the specific situations.

The one market distinction: **the forms record what happened, the adviser helps decide what happens next.**

Four public doors: owner tax and extraction, UK/international (UK/UAE first), exit readiness, accounts and financial control. Agency stays as a sector page, not the homepage.

Mechanism: **Diagnose -> Plan -> Maintain.**

## 2. The buyer

Not "small businesses." An owner whose company and personal money have started to collide:

- director juggling salary, dividends, pension, loan account
- profitable sole trader weighing incorporation
- anyone near VAT 90k, the 100k taper, pension taper, child benefit
- founder paying for accounts but flying blind month to month
- UK owner moving abroad, returning, or earning cross-border
- landlord entering MTD
- owner 1 to 5 years from a sale

Turnover is a weak proxy. Qualify on **decision complexity** and **relationship wanted**.

### Segmentation (buyer state -> best door)

| State | Trigger | Private fear | Door |
|---|---|---|---|
| Growing owner-manager | Profit and cash up | "I cannot tell what is mine" | Tax planning and control |
| Under-advised existing client | Accountant files, never initiates | "I find out too late" | Adviser-led accountancy |
| Internationally mobile | Moving, returning, earning abroad | "Two advisers, two answers" | UK/international |
| Exit-minded | Sale possible in 1 to 5 years | "It is worth nothing without me" | Exit readiness |
| Compliance-frustrated | Missed messages, deadline panic | "Nobody owns my account" | Direct Simon access |
| Software-capable price buyer | Does own books | "Am I paying for data entry?" | Clear responsibilities |

### Why they hire (triggers)

Threshold appears. Life event. Current accountant goes quiet. Surprise bill. Outgrew annual information. Software exposed the fee. Bank/investor/buyer raised the standard.

### Why they do not switch (kill these objections explicitly)

Fear clearance is hard, records and software access, outgoing accountant stalling, setup and catch-up fees, doubt the new service is actually different. So "we handle the switch" must spell out: professional clearance, software access, record transfer, opening balances, missing-work review, any setup fee. Before they ask.

### What "proactive" actually means to them

Contact before a deadline or decision. Issues raised they did not know to ask about. Options with consequences, not just rules. A known next review date. Business numbers tied to personal goals.

A **cadence beats the adjective.** Never write "proactive" without a schedule attached.

## 3. Buyer language bank (use these words)

- "I find out after year-end."
- "I want to know in the same tax year."
- "I do most of it in the portal."
- "I do not know what I am paying for."
- "Tell me what you need and when."
- "Will the old accountant hold things up?"
- "What is the business worth without me?"
- "I am getting conflicting advice."
- "I want it above board and legal."

Emotional jobs: control, timeliness, translation, ownership. "Peace of mind" is the symptom, not the headline.

## 4. Copy rules

**Strong territories:** before the money moves / know what is actually yours / the year-end meeting is too late / one view of company and owner / international without the fantasy / the exit starts in the books.

**Banned:** bespoke solutions, trusted partner, comprehensive services, stress-free accounting, maximise your wealth, tax made simple, we care about your business, proactive without a cadence, one-stop shop without boundaries.

**Needs evidence before it ships:** exact savings, client counts or years, response times, "specialist" in any technical area, PwC role and dates, review ratings, ICAEW/CIOT membership and logos, exit and UK/UAE experience, "direct Simon access" at every tier.

**Verbs:** model, assess, identify, plan, coordinate. Never guarantee a saving, a relief, or a residence outcome.

**UK/UAE framing:** not "move to Dubai, pay no tax." It is *know what still follows you from the UK before you move, restructure, invoice, or extract.* SRJ owns UK analysis, writes down assumptions, a UAE adviser confirms local treatment. Never imply SRJ gives UAE legal or regulatory advice.

**Exit framing:** "exit readiness" or "tax planning before a sale." Not M&A, not valuation, not negotiation.

## 5. Homepage sequence

1. Hero: timing mechanism, not a service list
2. Authority strip: CTA, ACA, ex-PwC, Simon direct
3. Current state: recognition, no attack on their accountant
4. Amplification plus solution, one compact block: records arrive late -> thresholds fix -> current numbers plus planned review -> owner acts knowing the consequence
5. Dream state: what is extractable, what is reserved, what the next decision changes
6. Four decision doors
7. Who this is for (qualify, no random occupation list)
8. Diagnostic first step (bounded, not free advice)
9. How it works, including switching
10. Services (completeness, outcome-led, links out)
11. Testimonials, different business types, **above** the articles
12. About Simon, tied to the proposition not a career chronology
13. Plain-English advice
14. Final CTA: name, email, business, situation, decision or deadline, plus what happens next

Restraint: the homepage does not teach tax. Education lives on decision pages.

### How they judge, in order

Category recognition -> competence -> relationship risk -> process -> proof -> price and scope -> safety.

Credentials without process feel remote. Process without credentials feels lightweight. Both without proof are just self-assertion.

## 7. Offer architecture

1. **Compliance foundation:** accounts, CT, filings, agreed bookkeeping/payroll/VAT split
2. **Owner planning:** scheduled performance, reserve, extraction, pre-year-end reviews
3. **Decision projects:** cross-border, incorporation, restructuring, exit readiness, complex one-offs

No fixed prices needed on site. Do publish **what changes the fee**: structure, number of returns, transaction volume, record quality, payroll and VAT complexity, reporting frequency, owners and personal returns, international facts, advisory work, catch-up required.

Value is not only tax saved: penalties avoided, cash reserved in time, owner time returned, an earlier decision, a bad structure not built, cleaner numbers for a lender or buyer, confidence to extract lawfully.

Do not compete with low-cost software-led compliance on price. Target the buyer stuck in traditional annual compliance.

## 8. Competitors, the short version

- **Crunch** from GBP 94+VAT/month. Do not put the same deliverable list next to it without explaining the advice difference.
- **Alto** excellent agency copy, fixed fees, 30-day switch. Keep an agency page that matches this recognition.
- **Sidekick** and **The Wow Company** win on accumulated content, benchmarks, community. Not beatable with volume, do not try.
- **Protax** shows a small general practice reducing purchase uncertainty with visible prices, a named professional, third-party reviews.
- **UK/UAE firms and cross-border boutiques** have immediate corridor relevance. SRJ separates on conservative UK-led analysis plus full-practice continuity.

Never claim scaled firms cannot advise, never attack them. Show the structural difference: named adviser, agreed response route, planned reviews, clear line between processing staff and Simon's judgment.

## 10. Blocked on Simon

- **Money:** minimum and target recurring fee, preferred complexity range, whether low-complexity sole traders and landlords are wanted, geography, record-quality requirements, sectors refused
- **Service:** what the recurring foundation includes, where Simon is personally involved, review cadence, response expectations, onboarding and catch-up fees, what is project-priced
- **Spikes:** exact UK/UAE and wider international experience, foreign adviser relationships, written-advice and insurance boundaries, exit experience and partners, whether Exit Readiness and Owner Tax Reviews can be productised
- **Proof:** correct CTA/ACA/PwC wording with directory links, testimonial permissions, outcomes usable as cases, review profiles and request process, discovery call length and qualification bar, what the prospect gets after the call, what the first paid step is, which enquiries get redirected

Structure can be final now. Claims cannot.
